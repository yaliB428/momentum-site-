Why Cyprus

One of the world's most attractive markets for real estate investment

A European market, accessible entry prices, and demand coming from several directions at once.

01

Easy to reach

A short 40-minute flight makes it easy to get to the property and stay close to your investment.

02

A European market, in euros

Cyprus is a member of the European Union, and offers exposure to a European property market and a strong euro-based economy.

03

Cyprus is expected to join Schengen

Cyprus is working to complete its entry into the Schengen Area. Joining is expected to strengthen the country's accessibility and appeal, and to support demand for property from foreign investors.

04

Residency routes

There are residency routes for non-EU citizens, including permanent residency for investors who meet the conditions and the required investment amount.

05

Accessible entry prices

A range of projects and properties at prices that are accessible compared with Europe's main markets.

06

Tourism and international demand

Strong tourism, alongside families, students, workers, and international companies, supports demand for property.

Schengen

What is the Schengen Area, and why does it matter?

The Schengen Area allows travel between member countries without routine internal border checks. Cyprus joining is expected to strengthen its connection to Europe and make travel between Cyprus and Schengen countries easier.

01

Better access to Europe

Easier, simpler travel between Cyprus and Schengen countries.

02

Stronger international appeal

A deeper European connection for tourists, business people, and international residents.

03

A closer tie to Europe

Another step in Cyprus's integration with Europe.

The market in Cyprus

A growing market

€6.5B

Record deal value in Cyprus

The value of real estate deals in Cyprus reached €6.5 billion in 2025, up 8% from €6.0 billion in 2024.

25,600

Real estate deals in 2025

The number of deals in Cyprus grew 4%, from 24,700 in 2024 to 25,600 in 2025.

+16%

Rising demand from foreign buyers

Demand from foreign buyers grew 16% in 2025, with Paphos, Larnaca, and Limassol together accounting for about 80% of the growth.

€4.5B

Homes, the leading market

Residential property reached a deal value of €4.5 billion in 2025, 69% of the whole market.

My story

To understand the market, you have to be on the ground.

Itai Hazan

It all started with family real estate deals that brought me to Paphos. The deeper I went into the market, the more I saw the change the city is going through and the potential in certain areas, and I decided to move there and get to know the market from the inside.

For a year I lived Paphos day to day. I learned the neighborhoods, purchase and rental prices, where the demand is, the new projects, and the areas that are developing. Along the way I came to know the leading companies, the developers, and the attractive projects in the market in depth.

My work expanded to Limassol too, where I accompanied and sold apartments and deepened my knowledge of the local real estate market.

I don't show a client every project that comes my way. Every deal I choose to present is examined in depth: the company and developer behind it, the location, the purchase price against the area, the rental market, planned development nearby, and the numbers of the deal.

From a wide supply of projects and apartments, I filter and choose the deals that I believe truly deserve a look, so the client doesn't have to go through hundreds of options and can focus on the ones that have already been filtered and checked.

Less noise. More time on the ground, more checking, more numbers.
